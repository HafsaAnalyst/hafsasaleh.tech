For years my work lived in other people's houses. Code on GitHub, posts on LinkedIn,
notebooks in Colab, a CV in a Google Doc that I emailed around as a PDF. All of it
findable, none of it *mine* — and none of it telling a single coherent story about
what I actually do.

So this site exists to fix that.

## What you'll find here

- **[Projects](/projects/)** — the models, dashboards and analyses I've built, each with
  the problem it solved and the result it produced.
- **[Research](/research/)** — published papers and preprints, with code where it's public.
- **Articles** — this section. Notes on data science, mostly written for the version
  of me who was stuck on the same thing six months ago.

## What I write about

Three things, roughly:

1. **The unglamorous middle.** Everyone writes about model architectures. Almost nobody
   writes about the two weeks you spend discovering that a timestamp column has three
   different timezone conventions in it.
2. **Analysis that changes a decision.** A model with a good AUC that nobody deploys is a
   hobby. I'm interested in the gap between "technically correct" and "actually used."
3. **Working notes.** Short pieces on tools, techniques and mistakes.

## A note on frequency

I'd rather publish something considered once a month than something thin every week.
If that suits you, the [RSS feed](/rss.xml) is the reliable way to follow along.
